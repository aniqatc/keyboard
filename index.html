Did you know…

There are eight keyboard themes. Press F1 to F8, tap the matching keys, or pick one here. The shuffle key at the top right picks one at random.

For a colorful surprise, press one of the light-show keys:

  • F9 Wave: lights travel across each row
  • F10 Ripple: a rainbow spreads out from the center
  • F11 Sweep: a band of light crosses left to right
  • F12 Twinkle: random keys sparkle

The text field supports full Markdown. Switch between Write, Split and Preview, then copy, save or clear your note. Turn on fn and tap a key with a small label for a shortcut, like B for bold or 1 for a heading. Ctrl/⌘ + B, I and K work too.

Your browser may keep F11 and F12 for itself. Tapping the keys always works.
